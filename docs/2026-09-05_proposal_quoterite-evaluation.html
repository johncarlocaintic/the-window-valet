Quoterite Evaluation

A look at Quoterite as a possible replacement for MyBlindCo, and what still needs answering before deciding

Prepared for Josh LeClair by John Carlo Caintic · Marketing Strategy & Automation · September 5, 2026

This covers what Quoterite actually is, how it compares to MyBlindCo, what would need to happen to connect it to GHL, and the specific questions worth settling on the demo call with their owner. Nothing here is a recommendation to switch yet, that decision depends on answers only the demo can provide.

1. What Quoterite Actually Is

An AI-powered quoting and order platform built specifically for the window covering industry, blinds, curtains, drapery, awnings, not a general-purpose CRM or quoting tool bolted onto this industry as an afterthought. It covers the same ground MyBlindCo does today: quote generation, client management, job tracking, inventory, invoicing, and payments, all in one place. Its own AI features, live in the product today rather than promised for later, include voice-powered search across jobs, automatic meeting transcription and summaries, AI-drafted client emails, an AI quote checker, and AI-assisted price-list entry.

2. How It Compares to MyBlindCo Directly

Quoterite publishes its own comparison against MyBlindCo, and a few concrete facts are worth knowing. MyBlindCo's own accounting connection is limited to QuickBooks Online or Desktop only, no Xero or MYOB support. MyBlindCo's payment processing runs through its own "MyBlindCo Pay" via Stax, at a 2.6 to 3.5 percent transaction fee. Quoterite, by comparison, connects to QuickBooks, Xero, and MYOB as standard, and says it can link to any accounting system on request. Quoterite's own headline claim against competitors generally, including MyBlindCo, is working AI features today versus none, and a stated commitment that customer data stays exportable at any time rather than locked in.

3. Pricing

The tier that fits a retail and installation business this size is Advanced Retail Solutions, priced in the US at $195 a month plus $110 a month for each additional user beyond the first. A setup fee applies but the exact US amount wasn't confirmed publicly, worth asking directly on the demo call.

4. Connecting It to GHL, the Honest Picture

There is no confirmed way to connect Quoterite to GHL today. Quoterite's own marketing lists integrations with HubSpot, Pipedrive, and Salesforce as CRM connections, GHL is not mentioned anywhere, including a page where they list every integration they're proud of against a competitor. No public Zapier app was found for Quoterite (checked directly, it doesn't exist yet as a self-service option), and no public API documentation or developer portal was found anywhere either. Whatever connection ends up being possible would run through Quoterite's own custom-integration service, built and maintained by their team, not something set up independently. Cost and timeline for that are not published anywhere.

This matters because it's the same shape of problem MyBlindCo already causes, being boxed into whatever a vendor is willing to build. Quoterite's accounting integrations run in parallel to, not in conflict with, the QuickBooks-GHL connection already being set up separately.

5. What's Still Unverified

  • No free trial, demo account, or sandbox was found anywhere on their site, worth setting that expectation rather than assuming one exists to test with before committing.
  • Independent reviews are essentially nonexistent. One listing shows a rating with zero actual written reviews behind it, and no meaningful discussion was found anywhere else online. This is either a very new product or a very small customer base so far, either way, there isn't much outside proof yet beyond their own claims.
  • What a MyBlindCo-to-Quoterite migration actually involves, timeline, cost, what transfers and what doesn't, isn't documented publicly anywhere.

6. What to Settle on the Demo Call

Six questions are worth asking directly: whether a GoHighLevel connector already exists in any form; if not, what a custom one would realistically cost and take to build; what data Quoterite actually exposes for something like Zapier to work with, quote created, quote status changed, order status changed, and so on; whether a trial, demo account, or sandbox exists to test with real products and pricing before committing to anything; what migrating existing MyBlindCo customer, quote, and order history actually involves; and whether the Advanced Retail Solutions tier already includes CRM integration work or whether that's a separate cost.

One thing worth asking for as a live demonstration, not just a question: ask Quoterite's owner to show, on the call, a quote created in Quoterite triggering something, anything, inside GoHighLevel. If they can show real data moving from one system to the other in front of you, that settles the integration question immediately. If they can't show it, that's the answer too, and worth knowing before any commitment is made.

7. Where This Leaves Things

Quoterite looks like a genuine, purpose-built alternative to MyBlindCo on the quoting and operations side, with a real edge on AI features and a stronger stated commitment to not locking data away. What isn't yet answered is the one thing that actually matters for how this business runs day to day, whether it can talk to GHL at all, and if so, what that would cost and how long it would take. That's not a reason to rule Quoterite out, but it is the reason nothing should be decided before the demo call answers it directly.
