SEO & Paid Ads Vendor Options

1. Why This Is Worth Deciding Soon

Shade Strategy's engagement ends September 21st, and they currently handle the website, SEO, and paid ads. Nothing should sit unmanaged after that date, these two options below are both built specifically for contractors in home improvement trades, not generic marketing shops learning the industry on the job.

2. FatCat Strategies

Works exclusively with home improvement contractors, and window and door installers are explicitly one of the trades they name as a specialty, alongside remodelers, roofing, and custom builders. Covers local SEO, Google Business Profile management, Google and Facebook paid ads, website design and upkeep, and email retargeting. Their own pitch centers on getting contractors off dependency on shared lead aggregators and onto exclusive leads tied to real call tracking and ROI reporting, not vanity metrics. Reads as the better fit for a business this size, a smaller, more focused shop that positions itself as a partner rather than just a vendor.

3. Valve+Meter

A larger, broader agency that also names windows and doors specifically among the trades it serves, alongside HVAC, roofing, electrical, and several others. Offers a wider service menu, SEO, paid search, Google Local Services Ads, paid social, geofencing, streaming and display ads, direct mail, and multi-location support. Same emphasis on tracking spend to actual booked jobs rather than raw activity. Worth considering if there's interest in a bigger, more full-service operation, but likely more agency than a single-location business like this one needs day one.

4. The Practical Difference

Both explicitly work with window and door contractors, so neither is a stretch fit. FatCat reads as the leaner, more directly comparable option to what Shade Strategy has been doing. Valve+Meter brings a larger toolkit, useful if there's an appetite to expand marketing channels beyond SEO and paid search, but likely more than is needed to start.

5. Recommended Next Step

Valve+Meter offers a free marketing assessment with no commitment attached, worth booking that call directly. FatCat's site points to a pricing and lead calculator rather than a named free assessment, worth reaching out directly to ask what their intake process looks like. Compare what each proposes for this specific business before signing anything, and have both conversations before Shade Strategy's engagement actually ends, not after.
