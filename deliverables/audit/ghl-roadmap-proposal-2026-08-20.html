GHL Transformation Roadmap

The Window Valet

An 8-week plan to turn GHL into the business's central nervous system
Prepared for Josh LeClair by John Carlo Caintic · August 20, 2026

The goal is simple to state and hard to fake: every lead, sale, and customer should move through one clean, dependable system, and you should be able to see what's happening without asking anyone. We're getting there in eight focused phases, one objective at a time, so we fix what's broken before we build what's new.

Why this order

Building reporting on top of a broken foundation just produces confident-looking wrong numbers. Adding AI on top of inconsistent workflows just automates the inconsistency. So the sequence is deliberate: stabilize first, then build one clean process, then make it visible, then connect the outside systems, then optimize, then automate further. Each week has exactly one objective, and anything we discover along the way that isn't urgent gets logged for its proper week instead of derailing the current one, that's what keeps this moving instead of sprawling.

How you'll stay in the loop

A short daily update on what moved, plus a fuller Friday report: what was planned, what got done, what didn't and why, what we found, and anything that needs your call. Anything urgent, lost leads, lost revenue, or a security issue, gets flagged to you immediately rather than waiting for Friday.

What we need from you: a green light to close out Week 1 this week and move into Week 2 next, plus your sign-off on the tag standardization piece above. Everything else is already in motion.
