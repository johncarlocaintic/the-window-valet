Josh approved an 8-week structured plan for building GHL into The Window Valet's central CRM and automation system, integrating MyBlindCo, Mapsly, QuickBooks, and future AI tools. Week order: 1) Stabilize/clean existing GHL, 2) Lead management & sales process, 3) Reporting/accountability dashboards, 4) MyBlindCo integration architecture, 5) Scheduling/geo-efficiency research, 6) Scheduling prototype build, 7) Partner (Trade Partner) system, 8) AI & advanced automation opportunities.

**Currently active: Week 1 (Stabilize and Clean GHL).** Checklist: complete remaining workflow audit; finish Drip/Nurture exit fixes; review/activate/fix the "Not Booked" workflow; confirm non-booking Facebook leads get proper follow-up; complete webhook security/validation; resolve the Partner New Leads Opt-in "Drip" tag question; verify round-robin assignment is actually functioning; review duplicate contacts and establish a safe merge process; prevent unnecessary duplicate opportunities going forward; begin standardizing tags; identify obsolete drafts/workflows for eventual archiving; test the full lead journey after changes land.

**Operating rules for every week:** one primary objective, required tasks supporting it, testing before deployment, documentation of changes, end-of-week results, and a proposed objective for next week. Don't chase every new idea mid-week.

**New-idea triage rule:** if something discovered mid-week is critical (lost leads, lost revenue, customer problems, security problems, or incorrect data), fix or escalate it immediately, same as the "Not Booked Workflow" and Drip/Nurture-loop findings were surfaced to Josh right away. If it's not critical, log it to an Improvement Backlog with: problem discovered, recommended solution, expected benefit, estimated difficulty, priority, and recommended week for implementation, rather than acting on it immediately.

**Friday Report format (weekly, separate from the daily EOD):** what was planned, what was completed, what wasn't and why, what was improved, problems discovered, new ideas/recommendations, items needing Josh's decision, proposed priorities for next week.

**Why:** This is the governing structure Josh set for the whole multi-week engagement. Work should be evaluated against the current week's single objective, not scattered across all 8 weeks' tasks at once.

**How to apply:** Before starting new work, check which week's objective it actually belongs to; Week 1 items take priority over anything from Weeks 2-8 right now. Use the bullet-form EOD ([[feedback_josh_slack_narrative]]) for daily updates and the fuller Friday Report structure for the weekly one. Route newly-found non-critical issues to a backlog instead of immediately chasing them.
